The remaining substitution details matter:

- **[INF] Coil wiring:** buy **AWG22** for the listed VH contacts; their specified wire range is AWG22–18. Twist each outgoing/return pair yourself. AWG24 is outside that contact’s published range.
- **[OBS] Crimp tools:** ENGINEER lists PA-21 compatibility with SVH-21T-P1.1. I found no verified Akihabara crimper-rental service. Preterminated leads provide the lower-cost route. [Manufacturer compatibility table](https://www.nejisaurus.engineer.jp/_files/ugd/104650_594335d26d724883affcaa8929df7c19.pdf)
- **[INF] Fuse:** MF-R300 needs a different footprint and has different protection behaviour from T3.15 A: 3 A is its **hold**, not trip current. Keep that distinction in the schematic/BOM.
- **[INF] NTC:** 103JT-025 is **not** the specified B3950 part. It requires the correct resistance/temperature conversion and temperature-trip validation.
- **[INF] HDD screws:** 6 mm is close to ¼″, but board thickness consumes that length. Select final screw length from board thickness and the HDD’s permitted thread engagement.
- **[INF] MDF:** it can provide the mounting board, but its acoustic response will differ from plywood. コーナン ドイト後楽園店 is the nearest practical lumber-store fallback I verified; exact plywood stock remains unconfirmed.
