A few substitutions require particular care:

- **UART power-off behavior:** [OBS] HD74LVC14 specifies input tolerance with VCC down to zero and input leakage up to ±5 µA over its stated temperature range. [INF] Two cascaded gates preserve the original non-inverting Schmitt function for this receive-only node. This is **input power-off tolerance**, not a claim that its outputs have general Ioff protection. [Renesas datasheet](https://www.renesas.com/en/document/dst/hd74lvc14-datasheet?language=en)
- **Strict Ioff alternative:** [OBS] 秋月 also lists **74LCX541FT, 114112, ¥130/5**, with an Akihabara table showing one pack; its datasheet explicitly provides power-down protection on inputs and outputs. This last check was through web extraction rather than the live browser, so I would conservatively treat its quantity as **B pending a fresh check**. [INF] It is not Schmitt-triggered and specifies fast input transitions, so it requires checking the SmartNet bus edges rather than treating it as an unconditional replacement. [Product](https://akizukidenshi.com/catalog/g/g114112/), [datasheet](https://akizukidenshi.com/goodsaffix/74LCX541FT_datasheet_ja_20181030.pdf)
- **74AHC1G125W5:** [OBS] The stocked Diodes Incorporated part specifies input leakage with **VCC = 0–5.5 V** and Schmitt action. [INF] It is a compact receive-only alternative with `/OE` grounded, but retain its input-transition limits and do not infer full output-Ioff behavior from “AHC.” [Datasheet](https://akizukidenshi.com/goodsaffix/74ahc1g125.pdf)
- **LED shifter:** [OBS] TC74HCT244AP has a guaranteed **2.0 V minimum HIGH-input threshold**, unlike ordinary 5 V HC logic. [INF] In USB-only operation, hold Pico LED data low whenever the buffer’s 5 V supply is absent, to avoid input-clamp backfeeding. [Toshiba datasheet](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/info/docget.jsp?did=16951&prodName=TC74HCT244AP)
- **Pickup diodes:** [OBS] Toshiba specifies **1SS307 leakage ≤10 nA at 30 V, 25 °C**. [INF] This is a credible low-leakage substitute here; its 30 V reverse rating is adequate for clamps across a 5 V supply, with the existing series resistor limiting transient current. It is not pin-compatible with BAV199. [Datasheet](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/info/1SS307_datasheet_en_20210625.pdf?did=3301&prodName=1SS307)

For the recommended **Pololu #4035 build**, these are the v0.4 changes I would make [INF]:

1. **Replace four bare-driver footprints with carrier sockets.** Keep local bulk capacitance, motor connectors, input pull-downs and the existing stop switch. Four carriers cost **¥8,720**.
2. **Set PMODE to 3.3 V before releasing SLEEP.** Remove the carrier’s **R4, 20 kΩ IMODE pull-down**, to obtain v0.4’s floating-IMODE configuration.
3. **Remove R2, the 10 kΩ SLEEP-to-VREF resistor.** Otherwise the carrier’s default connection interferes with the Pico-controlled VREF network and ARM loading.
4. Either **replace R3 with 4.99 kΩ**, reproducing v0.4’s nominal scaling, or leave its **2.49 kΩ** resistor and redesign the divider/calibration. With 4.99 kΩ, the existing 10 kΩ/5.1 kΩ divider gives approximately **0.496 A**. With the factory 2.49 kΩ, that same divider gives approximately **0.99 A**, **not 0.50 A**.
5. Keep the common **SLEEP/ARM** and wired-OR **FAULT** connections. No new eight-channel AND-gate bank is necessary.

The carrier resistor identities and connections are documented in the [Pololu schematic](https://www.pololu.com/file/0J1862/drv887x-single-brushed-dc-motor-driver-carrier-schematic.pdf). The current calculation uses TI’s **450 µA/A** scaling. [DRV8874 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8874.pdf)

[INF] A lower-rework option is to leave R3 at 2.49 kΩ, change the VREF divider’s lower resistor to **2.0 kΩ**, and recalibrate CS to approximately **1.12 V/A**. That produces about **0.491 A nominal**. R2/R4 removal is still needed to reproduce the intended configuration. These are nominal limits; tolerances, blanking and the sensing filter still matter.

[INF] **Pololu #4036 is a reasonable cheaper alternative**, costing ¥7,120 for four. Its current mirror is **1,000 µA/A**, so do not reuse the DRV8874 resistor calculations. Keeping its factory 2.49 kΩ and v0.4’s divider gives approximately **0.448 A nominal**, a conservative starting point. Its CS calibration becomes approximately **2.49 V/A**. [DRV8876 current regulation](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8876.pdf)

If four Pololu carriers cannot actually be obtained, I would use **four 秋月 AE-TB67H450 modules**, not bare TB67H450s. The modules cost **¥1,280 for four**, but require these changes [INF]:

1. **Add four current-setting shunts.** Connect each RS pin through its own **0.22 Ω low-inductance resistor** to local power ground. The relation is  
   **Itrip ≈ VREF / (10 × RRS)**.  
   With v0.4’s approximately 1.115 V maximum VREF, this gives **0.507 A nominal**. Recalculate tolerances and select resistor power rating; 0.5 A through 0.22 Ω dissipates about **55 mW**.
2. **Replace the IPROPI measurement circuit.** There is no proportional current-output pin. RS is a switching shunt node, including possible negative excursions; it needs suitable filtering, protection and preferably amplification before the mux. Its filtered value is duty/decay dependent and is **not an interchangeable IPROPI or RMS measurement**. Retain the external current-probe validation method.
3. **Implement hardware ARM gating:**  
   `driver INx = Pico INx AND ARM_after_stop_switch`.  
   Eight inputs require **two 74HC08 DIP14s**, powered at **3.3 V**, with input/output pull-downs and bypass capacitors. 秋月 **114059** was **¥30, 61 units, A**, at 11:37. [Product](https://akizukidenshi.com/catalog/g/g114059/)
4. **Remove the nonexistent nFAULT connection and revise fault handling.** TB67H450 has internal protections, but exposes no fault output. Coil NTC/VM monitoring cannot fully replace IC fault reporting; thermal shutdown can recover automatically. Preserve software fault latching for faults the system can observe.
5. **Retain 62.5 kHz initially, but revalidate the waveform and limiter.** The 400 kHz input limit is sufficient, yet the part specifies **500 ns minimum input pulses**, approximately **3.6 µs current-detection blanking**, and roughly **25 µs current-chopping off-time**. These are significant against a 16 µs PWM period. Frequency acceptance does not guarantee v0.4’s effective audio resolution or an instantaneous 0.5 A ceiling.
6. **Revise start/stop timing.** Use a definite standby interval of **at least 1.5 ms** when entering standby, and allow up to **30 µs** after wake-up. Avoid deliberately operating in the documented 0.7–1.5 ms standby-transition interval.

Those changes follow the [Toshiba datasheet](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/info/docget.jsp?did=65346&prodName=TB67H450FNG), [application note](https://akizukidenshi.com/goodsaffix/TB67H450FNG_application_note_ja_20190510.pdf), and [秋月 module schematic](https://akizukidenshi.com/goodsaffix/AE-TB67H450_schematic_20191120.pdf).

[INF] A **VM high-side disconnect** is another shutdown mechanism, but it must be a separately designed load switch. The existing reverse-polarity P-MOSFET orientation cannot simply become an effective forward disconnect: its body diode supplies the load when the channel is off. Also retain a local regeneration path, bulk capacitor and clamp downstream of any disconnect. For this build, the two 74HC08s are the more direct way to preserve immediate ARM control.

If **15 V operation must remain supported**, this report leaves the TVS sourcing item open; a generic 15 V zener or 45 V TVS is not an adequate automatic substitution.

Finally, **a custom PCB is optional with the carrier/module approach** [INF]. Pololu #4035 boards are approximately **17.8 × 15.2 mm**, so four do not by themselves force a large board. A layout near v0.4’s 100 × 80–100 mm target remains plausible, but has not been placed or verified. Bare TB67H450 saves module area while adding an exposed-pad assembly requirement and retaining the extra sensing/gating circuitry. I would prototype one channel on soldered perfboard, then choose PCB dimensions after placing the actual modules, connectors and capacitors. **Both Pololu carriers and AE-TB67H450 avoid student-performed exposed-pad reflow; Pololu resistor rework still requires small-component soldering.**

Assumptions: the initial target remains approximately **0.50 A nominal per channel**, the Pico is the original RP2040 model, and any fixed-12 V variant is a proposed specification change—not an assertion that v0.4 already permits it.
