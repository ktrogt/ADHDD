## New and remaining issues

### 1. Major — UART resistance limits current but does not establish compliance (§5.4, §7.11)

**[OBS]** At 3.3 V, RP2040 specifies \(V_{IL,max}=0.8\) V and \(V_{OL,max}=0.5\) V. Its IO absolute maximum remains IOVDD+0.5 V. [RP2040, pp.614–616][rp]

**[INF]** Including both series resistors:

\[
V_{RX,L}=\frac{10k\,V_{OL}+1.1k(3.3)}{11.1k}
\]

This gives **0.327 V** for ideal VOL=0, or **0.777 V** using VOL=0.5 V. Nominally it passes, although the latter leaves little tolerance/noise margin.

When unpowered, assuming no internal clamp and a dead 3.3 V rail, the external network alone gives **2.973 V at RX** and approximately **0.297 mA into that rail**. An assumed 0.3 V clamp would instead receive approximately **2.70 mA**. Neither model establishes permitted operation.

**Fix before procurement:** make a documented partial-power-down buffer mandatory, rather than an alternative to the resistor. TI’s SN74LVC1G17 explicitly supports powered-off input/output conditions through its Ioff feature. [TI §7.3][buffer]

### 2. Major — VM divider lacks surge margin and creates another unpowered-input path (§5.4, §6.8)

**[INF]** For 100 kΩ/15 kΩ:

- 12 V → **1.565 V**.
- 25.2 V → **3.287 V**.
- With 1% resistors at the adverse extremes, 25.2 V → **3.345 V**.

**[OBS]** ADC input voltage must not exceed IOVDD; exceeding it causes protection-diode leakage. [RP2040 §2.9.5, p.152][rp]

**[INF]** VM can be present before the Pico’s supply starts. Assuming a 0.3 V clamp with IOVDD off, 12 V produces approximately **97 µA** of injection. Reducing the divider ratio fixes normal surge headroom but does **not** fix powered-off injection.

**Fix before procurement:** design a front end explicitly safe with Pico power absent—potentially a suitably rated VM supervisor/comparator interface instead of direct ADC monitoring. Include tolerances, filtering and sampling latency.

### 3. Major — Power-component coordination remains incomplete (§5.5, T1)

**[OBS]** The Vishay P4KE18A specifies **25.2 V clamping at 15.9 A**. [Vishay, p.2][tvs]

**[INF]** “7–24 V input” plus “tolerates something near 25 V” is not a usable buck specification. The 35 V capacitors and TVS position are improvements, but the buck needs a documented rating above the actual clamp plus overshoot.

A **3 A time-lag fuse may never clear** a fault supplied by a current-limited 3 A adapter. This can be acceptable only if the adapter’s limiting behavior and downstream wiring make that condition safe.

**Fix:** select exact buck/fuse/diode parts and document fault coordination before purchase. For T1’s resistor check, specify a supply limit—for example **≤0.5 A at 12 V**, bounding sustained input power to **6 W**. An unrestricted 12 V across 10 Ω would be **14.4 W**. Also redraw §5.1 so the TVS clearly shunts VM to GND; its present ASCII placement resembles a series element.

### 4. Major — Restart/legato mathematics is correct, but excursion containment is conditional (§4.4, §6.2)

**[INF]** At an ideal mid-pulse turnaround, velocity is zero and the currently active force points inward. Keeping that polarity for **\(T_{\text{new}}/4\)**, then alternating new half-periods, is therefore correct.

However, the new travel span is

\[
D_{\text{new}}=\frac{|a_{\text{new}}|T_{\text{new}}^2}{16}.
\]

It remains within the immediately preceding excursion only when **\(D_{\text{new}}\le D_{\text{old}}\)**. An octave downward at unchanged force multiplies travel by **four**. Varying excursions can move the operating region across successive notes.

**Fix:** qualify the containment claim and add large pitch/velocity-transition tests. Define recovery from “direction unknown” after emergency stop. Also test VREF changes: its RC ramp means the assumed new constant acceleration does not start instantaneously.

### 5. Major — PIO is feasible, but needs an explicit safe execution contract (§6.2, §6.8)

**[OBS]** A 50 µs wake interval exceeds Toshiba’s specified 30 µs maximum. [TB67H450, p.6][tb]

The important PIO requirements are:

- **[INF] Pin takeover:** disabling an SM is not sufficient shutdown. Preload SIO output latches LOW and enable SIO output direction before changing the mux; prevent concurrent callbacks from restoring PIO ownership.
- **[OBS] Retained state:** `pio_sm_restart()` does not reset PC, OSR or X/Y. [SDK PIO source][pio]
  **[INF]** Abort DMA, clear FIFOs/pending work, and explicitly initialize entry point and registers before re-arming.
- **[OBS] Side-set:** it overrides overlapping SET/OUT writes and takes effect even when its instruction stalls. [RP2040 §3.5.1][rp]
  **[INF]** Audit every blocking PULL/PUSH/WAIT: starvation must produce 00 or preserve a balanced autonomous waveform, never freeze 10/01. A healthy CPU can otherwise keep feeding the watchdog while the SM remains stuck.
- **[OBS] Counters:** X/Y are 32-bit, but SET immediates only cover 0–31. [RP2040 §§3.2.3,3.4.10][rp]
  **[INF]** At 125 MHz and DIV=1, a 30 Hz half-period requires approximately **2,083,333 cycles**, easily within 32 bits. Load large counters through FIFO/register transfers, include instruction overhead, and handle decrement-loop off-by-one/underflow.
- **[INF] Accuracy:** at that clock, a 2 kHz quarter-period is **15,625 cycles**. Nearest-cycle quarter timing can achieve approximately **0.0554-cent** worst-case quantization across the proposed range, excluding clock error. Thus the accuracy goal is feasible, but not yet demonstrated by the unspecified program.

**Fix before coil drive:** implement and scope-test this contract, including starvation and repeated emergency-stop/restart cycles.

### 6. Major — T1’s RS RMS measurement cannot directly populate the thermal table (§6.7, §8)

**[OBS]** Toshiba’s slow-decay circuit circulates winding current through the two low-side switches. [TB67H450, p.9][tb]

**[INF]** That circulating current bypasses the external sense resistor. Consequently, **RMS(VRS)/RS is not full winding RMS current** and can understate heating.

Also, RMS and mean square are different quantities:

\[
P=I_{\mathrm{RMS}}^2R=\langle i^2\rangle R.
\]

**Fix before thermal calibration:** use a winding-series shunt with suitable differential measurement, or a current probe. Measure across relevant frequency/VM settings, or use a conservative envelope; a setpoint-only table is insufficient unless its validity is demonstrated.

### 7. Major — Thermal model equation is valid; boot and thresholds are not conservative (§6.7)

**[INF]** The first-order equation is dimensionally correct. With the default 35 K limit and 25 °C ambient:

- Boot at 50% means assuming **42.5 °C**, even if the coil is already at 60 °C.
- Hard stop at 120% means **67 °C**, exceeding G5.
- “Throttle” needs a defined power reduction sufficient to cool below the 80% release threshold.

**Fix before sustained tests:** use measured temperature or a conservative boot lockout/cooldown; place hard shutdown at or below the temperature limit and throttle earlier. Before Rth/Cth are calibrated, use explicitly bounded pulses and independent temperature supervision.

### 8. Major — VM recovery needs a precise freshness and detection rule (§6.8)

**[INF]** **350+100=450 ms is correct**, provided feeding immediately follows successful safety checks and remaining latency stays within the budget.

“New NOTE_ON” must mean one accepted **after stable VM recovery**, with stale UART/parser/queue/PIO commands excluded. A polling ADC can miss a brief VM interruption and therefore cannot guarantee inhibition after every power dip.

**Fix:** specify sampling interval, hysteresis, stable-good qualification and the re-arm boundary. If G4 covers arbitrary brown-outs, use hardware power-good inhibition/latching rather than relying solely on polling.

### 9. Major for firmware integration — §7 requests are improved but still omit several contracts

**[INF]** Items 1–12 are broadly appropriate, with these additions:

- **Items 1–2:** define the wire-time reference for synchronization and allowed execution error. §6.2’s nearest-turnaround policy permits **±8.33 ms at 30 Hz**, even with precise timestamps.
- **Items 5, 8–10:** explicitly handle late OFF/panic, STATE sequence coverage, stale sessions and PC loss while master heartbeats continue.
- **Item 7:** delta timestamps require an absolute resynchronization mechanism after a lost/corrupt frame.
- **Item 11:** a series resistor is not interchangeable with powered-off input protection.

**Required gates:** resolve issues **1–3 before finalizing the electronics BOM**; resolve **5–8 before ordinary powered coil testing**, with low-energy shutdown checks performed first. Mechanical transition validation in **4** can remain part of guarded T2 testing.
