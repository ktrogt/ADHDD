## Evidence conventions and access limitations

- `primary`: opened and read an official manufacturer document or the actual product's store page. A manufacturer datasheet hosted by the store is still a primary document; its host is identified below. Store descriptions are direct evidence of the advertised item, not proof of a measured specimen.
- `secondary`: a third-party description. No final `parts.csv` row uses this category.
- `assumption`: design choice, calculation, or unverified geometry, always identified separately from observed dimensions. Every recommended PCB pad/drill is a design recommendation rather than a manufacturer guarantee.
- Dates below refer to research on 2026-10-21. No purchase, message, public post, commit or push was made. Stock and prices were not part of this task.
