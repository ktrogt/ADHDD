## Questions to answer

- Mechanical modifications people do: open the cover? remove platters? remove heads/sliders? remove or keep the magnetic/inertial latch and crash stops? add mass or strikers? mount on a resonating board? keep the top cover on/off for loudness?

- Known projects to cover (find links): Floppotron (Paweł Zadrożniak, uses HDDs for percussion), Moppy/Moppy2 (does it support HDDs?), Japanese niconico/YouTube "HDD演奏", "HDDで演奏してみた", "ハードディスク 楽器" builds, Hackaday/Instructables "hard drive music", "HDD drum machine", "hard drive speaker". ### Q2. VCM electrical/mechanical characteristics

### Q3. HDD selection for an instrument

**1. Summary of findings**

- **[OBS][H] The head-positioning voice coil motor (VCM) is the principal sound-producing mechanism in the documented HDD instruments below.** HanDen explicitly identifies the head actuator as the source and describes vibration passing through the chassis into a wooden box. [HanDen, 2023-10-28](https://blog.handen.net/archives/33624449.html)
- **[OBS][H] The requested video identifies HDDs as the melody instruments.** Its description assigns bass to steppers, drums to solenoids, and cymbals to an iron, airbrush, and solenoid valve. Displayed publication date: **2026-09-12**. [Exact video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkKPoRMj-Wc)
- **[OBS][H] Floppotron uses HDDs for percussion:** electrical impulses move the arms into their stops. Its HDD method should not be mistaken for the tonal method in the reference video. [Floppotron, 2016-07-06](https://silent.org.pl/home/2016/07/06/return-of-the-floppies/)
- **[OBS][H] Tonal operation does not require stop impacts.** One Japanese build explicitly adjusts arm reversal to prevent collisions. [oykenkyu, 2019-09-09](https://oykenkyu.blogspot.com/2019/09/hdd.html?m=0)
- **[OBS][H] Documented drive electronics include single MOSFET switching, discrete full bridges, and audio amplifiers.** There is no single standard circuit. [名称未設定, 2023-08-15](https://qiita.com/misettei_meisyo/items/7c100fd3d42be19aa395), [Hard Drive Rock, 2019-12-13](https://pages.hmc.edu/harris/class/e155/projects19/Meike_Norfleet.pdf), [leevonk, 2006-10-13](https://www.instructables.com/More-Aesthetic-Hard-Drive-Speaker/)
- **[OBS][H] Mechanical conversion varies substantially.** Examples retain the platters, remove them, or cut away the platter portion of the housing. Platter removal is not a universal requirement. [HanDen](https://blog.handen.net/archives/33624449.html), [leevonk](https://www.instructables.com/More-Aesthetic-Hard-Drive-Speaker/)
- **[OBS][H] Acoustic mounting matters.** A speaker builder reports that placing the mechanism on a suitable sounding surface is essential; holding it in the hand produces little useful sound. [leevonk](https://www.instructables.com/More-Aesthetic-Hard-Drive-Speaker/)
- **[OBS][M] Individual reported VCM resistances include approximately 5.5 Ω, 7 Ω, and 36.8 Ω.** These are not a defensible “typical HDD resistance range”: model/form-factor identification is mostly missing. [5.5 Ω](https://forum.arduino.cc/t/moving-a-hdd-voice-coil/194861), [7 Ω](https://forum.arduino.cc/t/driving-10-hdd-voice-coils-and-need-for-choice-of-flywheel-diode/59186), [36.8 Ω patent measurement](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2000044084A2/en)
- **[OBS][H] Coil overheating is a demonstrated limitation**, including in a published repurposed-HDD actuator experiment. Supply voltage alone does not establish a safe operating condition. [Maguire et al., 2004-09-15](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1786331)
- **[INF][M] Best initial donor category: inexpensive, conventional air-filled 3.5-inch desktop HDDs with intact mechanics.** Accessible contacts and mechanical condition matter more than SATA versus IDE, because documented conversions bypass the original controller. [Hard Drive Rock](https://pages.hmc.edu/harris/class/e155/projects19/Meike_Norfleet.pdf)
- **[OBS][H/M] Akihabara evidence supports historical ¥500–¥1,000 options, but not an assumption of ¥100–¥300 availability today.** A photographed October 2026 offer is ¥2,000 for a junk 3.5-inch 1 TB drive. [2025 shop survey](https://js2y.com/akihabara-hdd/), [2026-10-10 price photograph](https://www.gdm.or.jp/sp20261010_si_b)
- **[INF][H] Avoid helium-sealed donors for this project.** Western Digital documents their laser-welded sealing construction. [WD, 2021-04-07](https://blog.westerndigital.com/race-to-seal-helium/)
- **[INF][M] Plan a room test and keep microphone/PA amplification available as a contingency.** The reviewed build reports do not establish that three or four drives will cover a classroom or contest hall unaided. [Mounting-dependent speaker example](https://www.instructables.com/More-Aesthetic-Hard-Drive-Speaker/)

**Mechanical conversion and sound modes**

- **[OBS][H] Tonal conversion example:** oyken removes PCB/lid, releases the parking restraint, removes the ramp where present, slightly lifts sliders to avoid rubbing, and reinstalls the main VCM magnet. Coil pads are identified through flex traces or resistance measurements; two wires are soldered on. The central three/four spindle contacts are different. Auxiliary coils provide reversal feedback. [oykenkyu, 2019-09-09](https://oykenkyu.blogspot.com/2019/09/hdd.html?m=0)
- **[INF][H] Parking latch and crash stop are different functional parts.** Releasing a latch can free motion; retaining a stop is integral to Floppotron-style impact percussion. “Remove every obstruction” is not a sound general instruction. [Floppotron](https://silent.org.pl/home/2016/07/06/return-of-the-floppies/), [WD teardown showing separate travel stops, 2023-04-08](https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/WD%2BBlue%2BHard%2BDrive%2BTeardown/159818)
- **[OBS][H] Platter/head treatment is build-dependent.** The speaker build removes the platter region; HanDen retains disks for appearance. The surveyed sources do not establish a universal need to remove all heads/sliders. [leevonk](https://www.instructables.com/More-Aesthetic-Hard-Drive-Speaker/), [HanDen](https://blog.handen.net/archives/33624449.html)
- **[OBS][H] Striker prototype:** Andy Murkin removed platters and restraints, then demonstrated battery-operated arm “clunks”: **9 V for his 3.5-inch donor; 4.5 V also worked for his 2.5-inch donor**. He proposed using the arms as external strikers and platters as chimes, but that article does **not** demonstrate a completed drum instrument. [Builder report, 2018-02-25](https://andymurkin.wordpress.com/2018/02/25/dismantling-a-hard-drive/)
- **[OBS][M]** No controlled cover-on/cover-off loudness comparison, added-mass optimization, or standard inertial-latch modification procedure was located in these reports.
- **[INF][M] Timbre expectation:** non-impact VCM excitation should behave as a mechanically colored speaker/buzzer; floppy and stepper music derives from repeated motor steps. Drive waveform, mounting resonances, and impacts can dominate the distinction. This is a mechanism-based comparison, not a measured listening test. [HanDen](https://blog.handen.net/archives/33624449.html), [Floppotron explanation](https://silent.org.pl/home/2016/07/06/return-of-the-floppies/)

**Voltage/current and overheating**

- **[OBS][H]** Music examples include **5 V drive systems** and HanDen’s **12–30 V board input**. These are implementation-specific values, not interchangeable donor-coil ratings. [Qiita](https://qiita.com/misettei_meisyo/items/7c100fd3d42be19aa395), [HanDen](https://blog.handen.net/archives/33624449.html)
- **[OBS][H]** The 2004 shutter experiment uses approximately **±100 mA holding current**, brief **30 V / 2.5 A** acceleration, and up to **±1 A sinusoidal excitation**. Operation at 100 Hz was restricted to short periods by coil overheating. **This is a shutter experiment, not a music-build recommendation or a 100 Hz musical pitch limit.** [Maguire et al.](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1786331)
- **[OBS][H]** An original HDD-controller example, ST’s **L7250**, uses an H-bridge current-control stage; its datasheet gives a typical application with approximately **2 A maximum coil current**. This establishes ampere-scale controller capability, not the actual firmware current of a particular donor. [ST datasheet, p. 27; undated copy](https://www.alldatasheet.net/html-pdf/22613/STMICROELECTRONICS/L7250/43843/27/L7250.html)
- **[OBS][H]** Toshiba describes **PWM current control during fast seeks** and **linear current control during precision tracking**. It does not give a universal coil-current figure. [Toshiba Review, 2023-01](https://www.global.toshiba/content/dam/toshiba/jp/technology/corporate/review/2023/01/a10.pdf)
- **[INF][H]** Continuous musical operation needs its own thermal/current assessment. Neither an HDD’s external 5 V/12 V power label nor a driver’s maximum output rating establishes a safe continuous VCM current. Basis: the differing implementations and overheating observation above.

**Travel, parking, and centering**

- **[OBS][H]** The shutter paper reports read/write-head travel up to **32 mm** in 3.5-inch drives and typical shutter sweep of **25–32 mm**. These are arm-end/sweep distances, not a universal linear coil stroke. [Maguire et al.](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1786331)
- **[OBS][M]** The patent’s measured donor is described as having **0.2 rad approximately half its travel**. **[INF][M]** That suggests approximately **0.4 rad / 23° total** for that example only. [Patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2000044084A2/en)
- **[OBS][H]** CSS parks the slider near the **inner disk diameter**; ramp load/unload parks it off the recording surface near the **outer edge**. [2010 dissertation, §2.3](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/85k909jm)
- **[INF][H]** There is no universal clockwise/counterclockwise “park direction”; geometry and electrical polarity must be established on the actual donor.
- **[OBS][H]** The modified actuator in the shutter paper has no internal spring or preferred rest position. Ted Sheflin adds a pivot potentiometer for positioning feedback. [Maguire et al.](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1786331), [Sheflin, 2023](https://hackaday.io/project/190947-hard-drive-voice-coil-speaker)
- **[INF][H]** A 50% PWM command is therefore not inherently a center-position command. Repeatable centering and generating an audible tone are separate requirements; simple tonal builds do not prove accurate open-loop positioning. [Positioning experiment](https://forum.arduino.cc/t/moving-a-hdd-voice-coil/194861), [single-MOSFET tonal build](https://qiita.com/misettei_meisyo/items/7c100fd3d42be19aa395)

**4. HDD selection**

| Shortlist | Recommendation and evidence level |
|---|---|
| **Conventional air-filled 3.5-inch desktop SATA HDD**, preferably inexpensive low-capacity stock | **[INF][M] First choice.** Documented 3.5-inch musical reuse, larger working envelope, and observed low-capacity junk offers make this practical. No evidence establishes that every 3.5-inch donor is louder than every 2.5-inch donor. [Hard Drive Rock](https://pages.hmc.edu/harris/class/e155/projects19/Meike_Norfleet.pdf), [Akihabara survey](https://js2y.com/akihabara-hdd/) |

**7. Spindle motor feasibility**

- **[OBS][H] Construction:** common HDD spindle motors are **sensorless three-phase BLDC motors**. Three terminals expose phases; some four-terminal examples expose the star/neutral point as well. The fourth terminal is not automatically a sensor connection. [T.K. Hareendran’s experiment, 2019-10-17](https://www.edn.com/hdd-bldc-motor/)
- **[OBS][H] Documented electronics:** Hareendran used a **DRV10866** with a salvaged Samsung 3.5-inch spindle and also tested a **5–12 V HDD motor-driver module**, identifying a **DRV11873** on the module. [Same report](https://www.edn.com/hdd-bldc-motor/)
- **[OBS][H] Another builder used a PIC18F4520 and TIP31C switching stages**, requiring gradual startup and observing loss of synchronism; reattaching platters helped as a flywheel. [Brad’s spindle experiment, 2010-02-04](https://bradthx.blogspot.com/2010/02/fun-with-hard-drive-spindle-motors.html)
- **[INF][H]** A compatible sensorless ESC or dedicated three-phase controller is the appropriate category. Compatibility and startup must be verified for the actual small HDD motor; “any hobby ESC will work” is not established by these examples.
- **[INF][H] Speed-to-pitch caveat:** 5,400/7,200 rpm correspond to **90/120 revolutions per second**, but an audible tone may occur at electrical or mechanical harmonics. “Audible frequency = rpm/60” is not a universal rule.
- **[OBS][H] A different sound mechanism exists:** researchers applied frequency-swept electromagnetic excitation to an HDD spindle winding to study its vibro-acoustic response. That is evidence of electrically excited spindle sound, not a demonstrated MIDI instrument tuned by rotation speed. [Research paper, 2009, DOI 10.1007/s00542-009-0778-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00542-009-0778-5)
- **[INF][M] Project fit:** spindle rotation is feasible as a separate visual effect or experiment. VCM excitation has substantially stronger documented precedent for this student’s three/four-voice musical objective. No convincing spindle-RPM-tuned HDD MIDI build was established in this survey.
